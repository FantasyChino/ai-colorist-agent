Upstream identifies LUMIX S9 Standard photo style with an sRGB base as the design
input. Treat use on arbitrary sRGB JPEGs as a preview experiment. A shared sRGB
encoding does not make every camera's already-rendered photo style equivalent.
The catalog's lower initial blend ranges are this project's conservative JPEG
policy, independent of the author's camera opacity recommendations.
