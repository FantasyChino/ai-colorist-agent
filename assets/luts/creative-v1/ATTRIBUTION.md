# AI Colorist Creative v1

Copyright (c) 2026 FantasyChino and AI Colorist contributors. MIT; see the root
`LICENSE`. These ten original recipes were authored with AI assistance and
baked by `scripts/build_creative_looks.py` without reading photographs or learned
source-image mappings. No pretrained neural model or photographer endorsement
is claimed. The exact tone/chroma/tint parameters and hashes are in `catalog.json`.

The tables have encoded sRGB input/output, fixed black and white endpoints,
continuous Lab adjustments and fixed-L* chroma compression at gamut boundaries.
The broad orange-hue attenuation is a color heuristic, not face/skin detection.
All are global artistic candidates; choose by actual image preview.
